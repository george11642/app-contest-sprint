## Required credit

**Keep this line on your end card.** The end card component in `src/hybrid/scenes2d.tsx` renders it by default:

> 3D characters: Quaternius (CC0 / CC-BY 3.0, via poly.pizza)

Full attribution: "Suit" by Quaternius, https://poly.pizza/m/sOUciDsoVV, CC-BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Materials are recolored in `src/kit3d/gltf/cast.ts`, and normals are re-smoothed at load time. Caps, badges, headsets, hoods and hair shells are procedural meshes in `src/kit3d/gltf/GltfCharacter.tsx`.

## Sound

All music and SFX are synthesized by `scripts/synth_audio.py`. No samples are used. The output is generated by setup and is not committed.

## Things you must check yourself

- **Remotion** has its own license. It is free for individuals and small teams, and companies above a size limit need a company license. See https://remotion.dev/license.
- **ElevenLabs** terms depend on your plan. Check commercial use and attribution for the free tier before you show the film publicly.
- **Fonts** load from Google Fonts through `@remotion/google-fonts`. Check any font you add.
- **Your own footage** needs fictional data, and every asset you add needs a license you have read.
